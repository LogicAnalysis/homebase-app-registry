About this App

AdGuard Home is a network-wide software for blocking ads and tracking. After you set it up, it covers all your home devices, preventing them from connecting to known ad, tracking, and telemetry servers without requiring client-side software.

It operates as a DNS server that re-routes tracking domains to a "black hole", ensuring a faster, safer, and cleaner web browsing experience across your entire local network.

Key Features:

  • Network-wide blocking: Protect all your smart TVs, IoT devices, smartphones, and computers simultaneously.
  • Modern DNS Protocols: Full support for secure DNS protocols including DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH), DNS-over-TLS (DoT), and DNS-over-QUIC (DoQ).
  • Parental Controls: Easily block access to adult content and enforce safe search on search engines with a single click.
  • Custom Filtering Rules: Create your own custom DNS rewrites or add thousands of community-maintained filter lists.
  • Detailed Analytics: Gain insight into your network's activity through a beautiful, easy-to-use web interface.
