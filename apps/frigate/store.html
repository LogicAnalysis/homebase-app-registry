About this App

Frigate is an open-source NVR (Network Video Recorder) built around real-time AI object detection. All processing is performed locally on your own hardware, meaning no cloud subscriptions are required and your privacy is fully guaranteed.

Instead of relying on generic motion detection that triggers on shadows or trees, Frigate uses machine learning to identify specific objects (like people, cars, or animals) with extreme accuracy, making it the perfect companion for home automation.

Key Features:

  • AI Object Detection: Accurately detect people, vehicles, pets, and more in real-time.
  • Optimized Performance: Designed to minimize CPU usage by using motion detection to trigger hardware-accelerated AI object identification.
  • Home Assistant Integration: First-class integration via MQTT for reliable notifications and complex smart home automations.
  • Smart Retention: Save space by configuring your recording rules to only keep footage when specific objects are detected in targeted zones.
  • Privacy First: 100% local processing with zero external dependencies or telemetry.
