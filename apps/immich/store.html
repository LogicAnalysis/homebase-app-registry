About this App

Immich is an open-source, high-performance, self-hosted photo and video backup solution directly from your mobile phone. It is designed to be an alternative to cloud-based services like Google Photos or iCloud, giving you complete ownership and control over your personal media.

Key Features:

  • Upload and view assets (videos and images) seamlessly.
  • Automatic background backup from your mobile device.
  • Facial recognition, location mapping, and object detection.
  • Multi-user support with sharing capabilities.
  • Extensive search capabilities based on metadata and machine learning.
