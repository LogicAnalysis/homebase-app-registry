About this App

Jellyfin is the volunteer-built media solution that puts you in control of your media. Stream to any device from your own server, with no strings attached. Your media, your server, your way.

Built as a completely free alternative to proprietary systems like Emby and Plex, Jellyfin ensures there are no premium licenses, hidden fees, or data tracking mechanisms separating you from your library.

Key Features:

  • Movies & TV: Hold your entire collection with automatic metadata and poster fetching.
  • Music & Books: Listen to your music library and read your ebooks or comics across devices.
  • Live TV & DVR: Watch and record live TV by setting up a tuner alongside Jellyfin.
  • SyncPlay: Share a watch party with your friends remotely.
  • Multi-Platform: Dedicated apps available for Web, Android, iOS, Android TV, Fire TV, and Roku.
